EntropyPixel

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This is the privacy policy for EntropyPixel ("the app"). It describes what data the app handles. The short version: the app collects nothing about you, and never sends anything to us on its own.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting SDKs, and no user accounts. We operate no servers, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

The app holds the internet permission for exactly one reason: the Google Play Billing library needs it to verify a Pro purchase with the Play Store. That conversation is between your device and Google — see Google's Privacy Policy. Nothing else in the app uses the network.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • Settings (grid size, simulation speed, mode, entropy value, and other app preferences) — so they survive restarting the app.
  • Run history (mode, board size, generations, elapsed time, completion date, and what the simulation settled into) for finished runs across the exhibits that keep one — shown in the in-app history lists.
  • Whether you own Pro, cached locally so the app works offline. The purchase itself lives in your Google account, not with us.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete the run history at any time with the in-app "Clear history" action, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Images

When you explicitly tap Save to gallery, the app writes the generated picture to your device's photo gallery. When you explicitly tap Share, the app hands the picture to the app you choose via the Android share sheet.

When you import a photo (a Pro feature), you choose one picture with Android's own photo picker. The app sees only that picture, never the rest of your library, and reads it on your device to turn it into a pixel target. The photo is not uploaded anywhere.

All three happen only on your action. The app never browses, scans, or uploads your photos.

On Android 9 and older, saving to the gallery requires the storage permission (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE); it is requested only when you use the save feature and is used for nothing else. Android 10+ needs no permission for this.

Other permissions

  • Notifications — only if you turn on *Run in background*: a notification shows while a run keeps going and when it finishes. Notifications stay on your device.
  • Run at startup — the app's icon changes daily, and Android forgets the scheduled change when the phone restarts; this lets the app re-schedule it. Nothing else runs at startup.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and contains no content requiring parental guidance.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example, by adding optional online features), this policy will be updated before that version ships, with the effective date above.

Writing to us

If you write to us from the app (the contact row on the About screen, or an exhibit's "Spotted an error?" link), your own mail app opens a draft pre-filled with the app version, your Android version, and your device model, so we can reproduce what you saw. You can read, edit, or delete that line before sending. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to reply and to fix the app.

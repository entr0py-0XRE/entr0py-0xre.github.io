Emergence

Order nobody designed

  • 01

    Chaos & Evolution

    Pure chance won't draw your picture before the heat death of the universe. Let the matching pixels stay, and the same picture shows up in seconds.

  • 02

    Life

    One rule, no goal, no target, and order anyway, on an endless plane. Zoom far enough into one board and you find Life running inside it.

  • 03

    Diffusion

    A gas spreads out with nothing pushing it. Add a force and the box un-mixes itself by weight.

  • 04

    Chaos Game

    Jump at random toward random corners, and the same fractal appears every time. The dice never mattered.

  • 05

    Galton Board

    Every ball falls at random. Together they draw the same curve, every time, and you can predict it exactly.

  • 06

    Langton's Ant

    Two trivial rules, ten thousand steps of mess, then a straight highway that never ends.

Paradoxes

Arguments you can settle

  • 07

    Monty Hall

    Switch doors and you win two times in three. Change how the host behaves, and the famous answer moves while the board looks exactly the same.

  • 08

    Birthday

    23 people make 253 pairs, which is why a shared birthday is likely. It still takes 253 people to have even odds that someone shares yours.

  • 09

    Simpson's Paradox

    Better in every department, worse overall. Drag one wall and watch it flip.

  • 10

    Boy or Girl

    Same family, different odds, depending on how you found out about the boy.
